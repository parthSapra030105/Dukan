# Vision

## The one-line version

Replace 70% of a kirana's phone-operator workload with a multilingual voice agent that knows the catalog, recognises regulars, and ships orders directly into the store's dispatch queue.

## Why now

Three forces converged in late 2024 / 2025:

1. **Voice AI quality crossed the threshold.** ElevenLabs (TTS), Deepgram (STT) and Vapi/Bolna (orchestration) now produce calls that customers don't immediately recognise as AI, especially in Hindi-English code-switched speech.

2. **Telephony unbundled.** Indian merchants can get a virtual phone number, hook it to a webhook, and route to an AI agent in <30 minutes. Five years ago this required a telecom partnership.

3. **Margin pressure on neighbourhood retail.** Zepto / Blinkit / Instamart took the 10-minute delivery layer. Local kiranas survived because of trust + credit + relationship — but their operator-led call ordering is now their highest-cost touchpoint per ₹ of GMV.

The first credible voice-AI vendor to package this for Indian retail wins a defensible market.

## What we're betting

That Indian D2C / retail voice AI is a category bigger than BFSI in 5 years, and:
- Inbound order-taking is the highest-frequency, highest-ROI voice-AI use case in retail
- Multilingual, code-switching support is the moat against US/EU vendors (who don't ship it well)
- A merchant dashboard with operator-grade workflow tooling beats a "developer API" for SMB adoption

## What we're NOT building

- A chatbot. This is voice-first. Text fallback exists but isn't the product.
- A replacement for human operators entirely. Escalation routing to humans is core, not optional.
- A multi-tenant SaaS for this submission. We build single-tenant first, with multi-tenant schema headroom for later.
- Payment processing. Orders are placed; payment is COD or merchant-handled OOB.

That perception is the deliverable.
